# Security Policy

## Supported Versions

We support the latest production deployment and the current `main` branch.
Older versions and deployments are not supported.

## Reporting a Vulnerability

Please do not report security vulnerabilities through public GitHub issues,
discussions, or other public channels.

Submit reports through
[GitHub Private Vulnerability Reporting](https://github.com/0xblackbot/rainbow-swap/security/advisories/new).
If you cannot use the private reporting form, email
[info.blackbot@gmail.com](mailto:info.blackbot@gmail.com).

Please include enough information for us to understand and reproduce the
issue:

- A description of the vulnerability and its potential impact
- The affected component, URL, contract, or commit
- Reproduction steps or a minimal proof of concept
- Any conditions required to reproduce the issue
- Suggested remediation, if available

Do not include secrets or personal data that are not necessary to demonstrate
the issue.

## Scope

This policy covers everything maintained by Rainbow Swap. Third-party services,
applications, and infrastructure that Rainbow Swap does not control are outside
the scope of this policy.

## Responsible Testing and Disclosure

When investigating a potential vulnerability:

- Use accounts and wallets that you own or have permission to test.
- Prefer testnet or an isolated environment when possible.
- Do not steal, destroy, or move funds or data.
- Do not access another user's data or degrade service availability.
- Do not perform phishing, spam, or social engineering.
- Stop testing and report the issue if you encounter sensitive data or a risk
  of harm to users.
- Use the minimum testing necessary to demonstrate the vulnerability.
- Do not disclose the vulnerability publicly until a fix is available and
  disclosure has been coordinated with the Rainbow Swap maintainers.

## What to Expect

We will acknowledge reports and provide updates as soon as reasonably possible.
Response and remediation times depend on the severity and complexity of the
issue. We may ask for additional information while validating a report and will
coordinate disclosure with the reporter when appropriate.

## Rewards and Credit

Rewards may be considered on a case-by-case basis. Submitting a report does not
guarantee a reward, and any reward and its amount are at the sole discretion of
the Rainbow Swap maintainers.

With the reporter's permission, we are happy to provide public credit for a
valid vulnerability report after remediation or coordinated disclosure.
